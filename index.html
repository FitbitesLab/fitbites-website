our story

we were tired of
choosing between
taste & health.

So we stopped choosing. Fitbites started as a cloud kitchen in Ho Chi Minh City with one obsession — food that's genuinely good for you and genuinely delicious. Both. Always. No exceptions.

We craft our Greek Yogurt from scratch daily. We bake our own granola. We develop every recipe ourselves — because if it doesn't taste amazing, it doesn't make the menu.
